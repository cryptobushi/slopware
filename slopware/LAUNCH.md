## Operations

**Keeper.** A Vercel cron (`site/api/keeper.ts`) runs every minute from wallet `0xB847754313D6320f43396F885d168b0B433b913f`. It completes any release past its deciding block, in batches of up to 40 via `completeMany`, with gas estimated on the chain and never assumed. It defers when the base fee exceeds `MAX_BASE_FEE_GWEI` (3) unless a release is within 20 blocks of losing its entropy, in which case it completes at any price. It fails loudly (a red cron run) when the wallet holds less than `MIN_BALANCE_ETH` (0.01). About 60% of collectors complete their own installs from the page before the cron reaches them. Environment variables on the Vercel project: `RPC_URL`, `SLOPWARE`, `KEEPER_KEY`, `CRON_SECRET`, `MAX_BASE_FEE_GWEI`, `MIN_BALANCE_ETH`. The key is readable by anyone on the Vercel team; the wallet holds gas money only.

**Site.** `slopware.fun` (also `slopware.vercel.app`), built by `site/build.mjs` with the installer address and RPC filled from the same variables the keeper uses. Reads go through cached functions, `/api/state` and `/api/release`, so the public RPC sees one read every ten seconds regardless of visitors; installs go straight to the chain through the collector's wallet. `/readings` is the lab's observation of every installed program, regenerated by `npx tsx src/readings.ts` and redeployed when releases arrive.

**Money.** `withdraw()` pays the artist only and may be called by anyone, including the keeper. The keeper has cost about 12% of sales at the launch price; at 0.001 ETH it breaks even on a walked-away batch up to roughly 8 gwei. When mainnet adopts the Amsterdam rules, installs and completions cost about four times more gas; `setPrice` is the only lever.

**Routine.** Top the keeper up from sales when its balance nears the alarm. Sweep sales periodically. Regenerate the readings when releases land.

## Still open

1. **Etherscan verification.** Needs an Etherscan API key; the Standard JSON input and constructor arguments are generated by `forge verify-contract --show-standard-json-input`. Sourcify is verified.
2. **No independent review.** The contract went live unaudited, by the artist's decision, with 21 tests and a 3,000-bytecode corpus. A review is still worth having.
3. **Proposer influence on entropy**, disclosed in `RANDOMNESS.md`, not eliminated.
4. **Keeper liveness is operational.** Down 51 minutes with nobody else completing means abandonment and a refund.
5. **`setPrice` is the one mutable thing**, and the gas-rule change coming to mainnet is the reason it exists.
